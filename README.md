# German revision resources

Original, unofficial German revision practice. Not endorsed by AQA. This is the public-safe collection, not a copy of every file in the private collection.

## Downloads

The original-practice ZIP contains 25 PDFs and one short prepositions audio summary. The newer photo-card pack is a separate download and is not inside that ZIP.

- [9 original photo cards: 18 licensed photos and 36 practice questions](https://github.com/tanyoganand-ops/german-revision-resources/blob/main/1-german-higher-photo-cards.pdf). Photo credits, individual licence links and resizing notice are on its final page. Photo licences do not apply to the original card text.
- [Original-practice ZIP](https://github.com/tanyoganand-ops/german-revision-resources/raw/refs/heads/main/1-german-original-practice.zip)

## Start here

- **Speaking:** the three `german-8662-...` PDFs contain adaptable frameworks, structural upgrades and a complex-language bank. They are the newer speaking resources.
- **Grammar:** Foundation grammar, Higher additions, a short complex-language guide and phrase frames. The prepositions crash course separates GCSE and A-level sections; it also has a short audio summary.
- **Listening:** original Foundation/Higher question papers, transcripts and answer keys, plus dictation practice. **There is no recorded audio in this collection.** Someone needs to read the transcripts aloud.
- **Reading:** original Foundation/Higher question papers and answer keys.
- **Writing:** original photo-sentence scenarios, 50-word tasks, gap-fills, translations, 90-word and 150-word tasks. The photo-sentence pack uses written scene descriptions, not photographs.

These resources were written in October 2026 for revision around German 8662. They are not official papers, predictions or guaranteed exam-standard assessments. Fictional first-person examples are practice answers, not biographical statements. Adapt them before use.

## Review limits

Teacher review is recommended before assessed use. Existing CHECK notes flag unresolved vocabulary-list membership and other review points. Mark allocations and suggested judgements in these original practice papers are guidance, not official grading. The older practice packs have not been fully re-audited for every language, timing or assessment claim in this public collection. Use the current official specification and your teacher's advice for exam requirements. The speaking language bank intentionally includes language beyond GCSE.

## What is not public here

- Official AQA question papers, specimen questions, reading-aloud texts, photo cards, mark schemes and cropped past-paper packs. This includes legacy 8668 material.
- Kerboodle/Oxford textbook full text, grammar screenshots, verb tables, extracted glossaries and textbook-derived word banks, including Foundation source archives and Higher spreadsheets.
- Bulk extracts of AQA's official vocabulary lists and decks built directly from those lists.
- Older superseded speaking practice with conflicting photo-card format claims, draft variants and broken placeholders.
- Raw source archives, private project records and working notes.
- Further annotated model-answer packs are held for additional review rather than presented as cleared.

No public redistribution permission for the excluded publisher/exam-board material has been established. The separate private backup was not made public or altered by this publication.
